## HTML5 named character references

`app/crates/core/src/jira/html-entities.json` is generated by `contract/record_html_entities.py`
from Python's `html.entities.html5` and the `html` module's invalid-reference tables, so the Rust
Jira renderer decodes text exactly as Python's `html.unescape` does.
